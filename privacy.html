Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026.

Rank Rush Uploader uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to the channel owner's own YouTube account. It requests the youtube.upload permission and nothing else.

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data from anyone. The only credential stored is the channel owner's own OAuth token, kept as an encrypted secret and used solely to upload videos.

Use of the YouTube API is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
